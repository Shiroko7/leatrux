In the sacred stillness surrounding [[Nilo]]'s secluded meditation for his adept breakthrough, [[Gio]] was visited during a dream by a serpent-like spirit. This entity congratulated him for passing his heart tribulation, conveniently omitting the profound question it had abandoned him with during that crucial moment.

The serpentine visitor issued a command: eliminate the [[The Goat|Goat]]. When [[Gio]] refused, expressing his favorable impression of the carnivorous creature, [[The Serpent]] appeared baffled.

"You truly do not see how the goat brings nothing but calamity upon your heads?" it questioned, scales rippling with indignation.

When [[Gio]] merely shrugged in response, [[The Serpent]] pivoted gracefully, assigning an alternative mission: fetch an egg from the "Immortal" [[The Chicken|Chicken]] instead. [[Gio]] accepted this task as if it were some sort of trivial side-quest, after all, he fully believes that this entity was someone to whom he was actually indebted. Before departing the dreamscape, the entity delivered a final instruction: "Take great care of my little descendant who will soon awaken," ensuring [[Gio]] understood this referred neither to himself nor to [[The Serpent|El Serpiente]].

Upon waking, [[Gio]] discovered a diminutive [[Serpent]] crawling atop his shell, already berating him over his alleged sale of its shed skin. When [[Gio]] denied this accusation, explaining he had merely attempted to bid on it, the little creature revealed a shocking truth: his revered masters had been the ones who auctioned away the skin!

"This is corrupted and immoral! How dare they?" the [[Serpent]] declared with righteous indignation.

[[Gio]] didn't fully believe his masters would do something like that. He also couldn't understand what was so important about a shed skin. The [[Serpent]] tried to explain how weird it would feel to have your shell sold, but [[Gio]] couldn't relate because that doesn't happen to turtles—although it literally just happened to him as he ascended to the adept level.

With dramatic urgency, the [[Serpent]] impressed upon [[Gio]] the dire consequences should the skin remain in unknown hands: "Someone might ruin my reputation by impersonating me!"

Though privately considering this too much of a reach, [[Gio]] nevertheless agreed to the quest. Thus, another unexpected member joined the crew.
