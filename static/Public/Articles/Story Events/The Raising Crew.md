"A lightning path supreme elder!?" "My knees... they are bending on their own!" "Is this the deva realm!?" Such cries erupted from the crowd as [[Lei Zhen]]'s overwhelming pressure suppressed everyone present by merely standing there. One by one, nascent soul supreme elders from the three great tribes appeared, their presences sharp as unsheathed blades ready to strike down this ancient intruder.

Most cultivators could barely remain conscious, let alone raise their heads to witness the gathering of such formidable beings. The tension was palpable as [[Zarvon Fungart]] continued questioning [[Lei Zhen]], who showed interest only in securing a place for his disciple. When the supreme matriarch of the [[Lluvia Tribe]], [[Selene Aeloria]], greeted him as her senior, [[Lei Zhen]] merely glanced at her before introducing [[Grommash Thunderfist]] as his disciple, announcing they had come to join a squadron for the [[Infernoheart Citadel]] raid.

[[Zarvon Fungart]] attempted intimidation, suggesting that their small group would struggle with only a handful of adepts. At this critical moment, two familiar faces stepped forward to stand protectively beside their disciples—[[Elira]] and [[Vanya]], taking a stance against their own superiors. [[Lime]] was the first to join them, followed by [[Harpy]]. [[Wang Dabao|Juan David De la Lluvia]] strode proudly toward the group, while [[Shui Yuan'hua]] arrived with [[Ces]] in tow. [[Fendral]] too had somehow materialized beside the party, having stood there for an unknown duration.

The party exchanged meaningful glances, silently recruiting more allies. [[Nilo]] caught [[Sister Mei]]'s eye, who seemed to have been waiting for the invitation and joined without hesitation. When his gaze fell upon [[Su Yaolan]], she apologetically shook her head, prompting him to look toward clan matriarch [[Su Ning]], who responded with an icy stare. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]  fixed an uncomfortably intense gaze on [[Lumina]], but she remained with [[Lady Elowen]]'s contingent. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] surveyed his [[Llamarada Tribe]] acquaintances, remembering his elder brothers aligned with [[Kaelan]]'s group while [[Chromaela Ironheart]] refused to meet his eyes as she stood with the [[Ironheart Clan]] patriarch. Only [[Marisella]] approached out of sympathy, though clearly reluctant to venture deeper.

As tension mounted between the supreme elders, [[Kazren Serpentsoul]] unexpectedly stepped forward, his gaze fixed on [[Zarvon Fungart]]. "I suggest you back away from this matter, [[Zarvon Fungart|Sorveign]]," he declared with surprising confidence. "My son [[Nilo|Nilo Serpentsoul]] stands among them." His tone carried an unmistakable threat as he continued, "I care not if you are the supreme sovereign—when it comes to protecting my clan's bloodline, there are no boundaries I won't cross." The audacity of challenging someone of far higher cultivation shocked onlookers, yet [[Kazren Serpentsoul|Kazren]]'s unwavering stance suggested he truly feared no consequences.

Many initiates and several adepts willingly joined their ranks, particularly [[Sister Mei]]'s followers from the [[Liberation Sect]]. Their assembled force swelled to over a hundred members, making them a league above in power with their collection of masters—though numerically inferior to the established clans.

As they discussed logistics, [[Durim Stonehammer]] emerged from his dissociative state, expressing distaste for entering such dangerous territory. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] pressed him to complete the ship before the expedition, asking where they should begin, when suddenly the earth began to shake and the heavens rumbled. Smoke billowed from the volcano as tribal envoys rushed to report to the supreme elders: [[Mu Youqing]] had breached the [[Infernoheart Citadel]]'s barriers, triggering a premature eruption. Alarms sounded throughout the grounds as supreme elders and masters departed to gather their squadrons, leaving the newly formed group alone as even [[Lei Zhen]] vanished from sight.

A new figure approached their gathering—[[Pyrenna Flamedancer]] from the [[Llamarada Tribe]], a celebrated idol and former [[Trial of Fire]] winner, noting they lacked a fire path master. Her status as a fellow [[The Shadow Sect|shadow sect]] member made her a valuable addition.

As newcomers exchanged greetings, discussion turned to leadership. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]  assumed a righteous stance and nominated himself as captain, only to be completely ignored as others suggested [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]], who declined, preferring to remain helmsman. The party recalled their original shipboard roles: [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] as helm assistant, [[Gio]] as lookout assistant, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] as navigator's assistant and cartographer, and [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]  as a one-day fisherman, prompting memories of [[Rudy Blagsta]], the fisherman head who still remains somewhere on the island. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] then promptly assigned himself navigator instead.

Consensus formed that their captain should be charismatic and well-known—qualities scarce among them. [[Nilo]] emerged as the leading candidate, having established relationships across all three tribes without a prior defined role. [[Sister Mei]] endorsed this choice, remarking that it was unfortunate [[Korrin Flameheart]] wasn't alive to present him with the special echelon robes that would suit him perfectly.

Seizing this fortuitous opportunity, [[Nilo]] revealed he had "happened to find" [[Korrin Flameheart|Korrin]]'s robes during the [[Trial of Fire]] chaos. He donned them officially before the assembled company, receiving respectful acknowledgment from new recruits while unspoken thoughts lingered in the air.
