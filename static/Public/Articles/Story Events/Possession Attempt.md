In the shadowed chamber where [[Nilo]]'s blood tribulation had begun, the world around him twisted into a darker reflection of reality. The aberrant dragon born of his [[Dragonmark]] loomed before him, its form composed of living darkness that devoured light itself. Tendrils of void essence reached toward him as [[Korrin Flameheart|Korrin]]'s spectral image materialized beside the creature.

"Your mark is not a curse but a gift," [[Korrin Flameheart|Korrin]] spoke, his voice resonating with unnatural clarity. "The power it offers could elevate you beyond mortal limitations. Why continue to resist your true nature? Embrace what you are meant to become."

[[Nilo]] stood defiant, his jaw clenched. "I've survived this long by my own strength, not by surrendering to this... parasite."

The dragon's maw opened in what might have been amusement, revealing an abyss where teeth should be. It approached [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] directly, guided by smell alone as it lacked vision. With frightening speed, it pierced her skin with its claws, holding her fast while its other taloned limb tore at her arm. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] fell unconscious from the first devastating strike, but the dragon was not finished. It bent down and bit into her belly, crushing her spine with a sickening crunch, leaving her body twisted in an unnatural position.

"I've come so far..." she whispered, blood trickling from her lips, "to just die in an instant?" Her gaze locked with [[Nilo]]'s for one final moment before the light within faded, leaving only empty vessels where vibrant determination had once burned.

"No!" [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] roared, his grief transforming into desperate action. The dwarf pulled forth an artifact of pure light, activating its magic with a word of power. Brilliant daylight exploded outward, illuminating every corner of the chamber.

The shadows shrieked, including those within [[Nilo]] himself. His skin blistered and smoked where the purifying light touched the darkness that had begun to infiltrate his being. The illusory form of [[Serenella Sunflare]] appeared across the chamber, her expression twisted with accusation and regret.

"I wanted to save everyone," she cried out, her ethereal form flickering in the daylight. "We could have tried together, but because of you, you doomed us all! You killed us with your own hands instead!"

[[Gio]] swung wildly at the shadows surrounding them, but his fists passed through them uselessly. In his frustration and confusion, he turned to [[Nilo]] and struck him directly, the impact jarring.

"Is this real?" [[Gio]] demanded, panic edging his voice. The pain of the blow convinced [[Nilo]] that this was indeed happening—though a distant part of him might have questioned why pain should be the ultimate arbiter of reality.

The dragon roared in fury, using its legendary action to launch itself skyward, dark wings spreading across the ceiling like a canvas of night. From below, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] summoned a second water clone, each version of himself attacking the remaining shadows with the [[Frostreaver]] since the dragon had risen beyond their reach.

The void wraith that had emerged from [[Elira]] earlier now glided silently toward [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]], its wailing intensifying. Before anyone could intervene, it passed through the dwarf's chest, draining his life force with terrifying efficiency. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] staggered, his skin graying and withering like autumn leaves in winter's first breath.

"Just as before," he murmured, recognition dawning in his eyes as death claimed him a second time. His gaze found [[Nilo]] one last time. "Keep on living, lad. Don't let it end here."

The dragon's wings folded inward as it exerted its second legendary action. A surge of void energy emanated from its form, creating a gravitational pull of impossible strength. The companions found themselves dragged toward a central point beneath the creature, helpless against this cosmic force.

With its prey gathered, the dragon unleashed its void breath—a torrent of nothingness that unmade everything it touched. The remains of [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]]'s daylight spell were extinguished. The bloodied floor, the echoes of their battle, even the wraiths themselves—all were consumed in that terrible exhalation.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] fell, the [[Frostreaver]] slipping from his grasp as the breath tore through his defenses.

"I couldn't protect them," he gasped, his eyes growing distant. "Forgive me, [[Selune]]... I won't be reuniting with you after all."

[[Nilo]] clung to consciousness by a thread as [[Korrin Flameheart|Korrin]]'s voice returned, insidious and persuasive.

"Do what you must to survive," he urged. "Consume everything, just as nature intended. Is there anything truly wrong with a living creature fighting to stay alive? Take what is yours by right."

Despite the temptation, a cold pain anchored in [[Nilo]]'s heart kept him from surrendering. It wasn't merely pain, but hatred—pure and refined, directed at the aberrant creature that had haunted him his entire life. His killing intent surged a hundredfold as he damned his [[Dragonmark]] for all it had cost him: his freedom, his choices, even his humanity.

"You made me a slave," [[Nilo]] snarled through bloodied teeth. "Everything that has gone wrong in my life traces back to you. No more."

From the corner of the chamber, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]'s second clone channeled energy into healing tides that washed over the fallen, granting them another chance. This clone behaved oddly, refusing to dissipate despite multiple attacks, as if influenced by an external force—perhaps [[Selune]]'s lingering presence providing aid.

Emboldened, [[Nilo]] reached out with his mind, seeking the connection between himself and the dragon. He could feel it—a tether of essence that bound them together. With a savage mental grip, he seized that connection and pulled.

The dragon howled, tearing reality itself as it made a desperate dash toward [[Nilo]]. Space bent in its wake, converting everything in its path to twisted fragments of matter. [[Gio]] caught the full force of this attack, his shell cracking like porcelain.

"I wish..." the tortle managed, his life fading, "we could have seen the world together. Perhaps one day, [[Nilo]], you'll see it for all of us."

After [[Gio]]'s death, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] suddenly opened his eyes, feeling [[Marisella]]'s warm hand upon his shoulder despite having been slain earlier. This phenomenon had occurred repeatedly throughout the battle—death followed by inexplicable resurrection, reality bending upon itself in ways that defied reason.

"Why do we always die first?" [[Marisella]] asked with a wry smile, helping him to his feet.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] steadied himself and channeled his remaining power into a final guiding bolt, the divine energy arcing toward the dragon to weaken its defenses.

[[Nilo]] felt himself slipping away as well, but [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]'s resilient clone reached him with another healing surge. Drawing upon the [[Heart Of Shadow]] and channeling everything into his heart-crushing technique, [[Nilo]] launched himself at the dragon in a last, desperate attack.

As his hand closed around the creature's heart, the shadows began to disperse like mist before dawn. What remained was not a monster but a reflection—[[Nilo]] himself, fragile and hurt, staring back with eyes that held no malice, only deep emotional pain.

Understanding crashed over him like a tidal wave. The shackles he had fought against had been of his own making. The dragon had never been separate from him; it was the manifestation of his own fears, his own self-hatred.

With newfound clarity, [[Nilo]] embraced his reflection. The [[Heart Of Shadow]] shattered by his decision to abandon the path of darkness, its purpose rendered obsolete. As the fragments fell, reality itself fractured around him, dissolving into nothingness.

He awoke with a violent gasp, finding himself in the middle of the ritual chamber, the final drop of blood trickling into his dantian to complete his foundation. Around him stood his companions, their faces etched with days of worry. His body had wasted away during the ordeal, skin stretched tight over bones, an aura of death clinging to him like a second skin.

[[Elira]] and [[Vanya]] moved quickly to his side, their expert assessment confirming what they had feared: something had attempted to possess him during his tribulation, and they had been powerless to intervene directly.

"We could only watch and hope," [[Elira]] whispered, relief evident in her voice.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] was the first to embrace him, followed by [[Gio]] and [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]]. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] joined them without hesitation, all previous barriers between them dissolved in this moment of shared relief. Their warmth penetrated the cold that had settled in [[Nilo]]'s bones during his ordeal.

"I've never felt weaker," [[Nilo]] admitted, his voice barely above a whisper. "And I've never been happier." He looked at his companions—no, his friends—with new eyes. For the first time, he recognized them not as tools for survival or temporary allies, but as precious connections worth protecting.

In that moment of profound weakness, [[Nilo]] had discovered a strength that his [[Dragonmark]] could never have given him.
