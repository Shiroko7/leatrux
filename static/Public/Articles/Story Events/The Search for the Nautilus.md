As the newly formed squadron began their descent into the fiery depths of the volcano toward the [[Infernoheart Citadel]], they opted for an alternative route suggested by [[Harpy]]. The fey creature, drawing upon fragmentary memories, guided them through a myriad of passageways and tunnels where she believed the [[Nautillus]] might be found. To expedite their search through the labyrinthine network of hundreds of tunnels, the group agreed to divide into five separate contingents, each led by a master.

The moment of separation brought forth the complex web of relationships and tensions within the expedition. [[Elira]] approached [[Nilo]], claiming him for her group with the casual possession of a master toward a prized disciple. Not to be outdone, [[Vanya]] seized [[Gio]], warning him with dark humor that should he perish, she would use his ashes to bake him into a cake. [[Ces]], the water master, attempted to persuade [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] to join him, emphasizing the safety advantage of traveling with a water cultivator. The fiery [[Pyrenna Flamedancer]] grasped [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]]'s arm, tempting her with a suggestive invitation to "get to know each other a little better." [[Sister Mei]], ignoring the neglected [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]], approached [[Nilo]] instead, playfully hinting at unfinished business and expressing the [[Liberation Sect]]'s desire to accompany him.

[[Wang Dabao|Juan David]] interrupted this territorial display, advocating that the party should remain together for mutual protection rather than being scattered to the winds. He proposed a simple vote to determine which master they would follow. When tallied, [[Nilo]] chose [[Elira]], [[Gio]] selected [[Vanya]], and [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] opted for [[Pyrenna Flamedancer|Pyrenna]]. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] decided to follow the majority, while [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] broke the resulting tie by siding with his tormentors, [[Vanya]]'s group, rather than the man who had repeatedly saved his life. With two dozen initiates joining their ranks, they set forth under [[Vanya]]'s leadership.

Their path led them through cramped tunnels until they emerged into a small cavern where ancient ruins stood testament to previous occupation. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]'s heightened senses detected the oddly pleasant aroma of beautiful flowers within, while [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]]'s keen eyes identified an old trap mechanism similar to those encountered during the third phase of the [[Trial of Fire]]. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]] examined the security trap, determining it targeted humanoids and had remained inactive for years—suggesting the current inhabitants were likely quadrupedal or small. He temporarily deactivated it to allow safe passage.

Lurking within the chamber was a massive [[Hellhound]], while a pack of eight smaller ones rested on the opposite side. The party devised what they believed to be the perfect strategy—using silence spells and plant spike growth, a tactic seemingly ill-suited to the barren volcanic terrain. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Gio|Gio]] was tasked with swiftly executing the lone sentinel outside the abilities' area of effect.

Their plan faltered immediately upon execution. The hellhounds proved far sturdier than anticipated, and though no sound escaped the silenced area, the pack survived the initial assault. They descended upon [[Public/Articles/PCs/Gio|Gio]] with ravenous fury, unleashing gouts of flame upon the entire party. Chaos erupted, the commotion rousing the dormant alpha female from the adjacent chamber. She leapt to attack [[Grommash Thunderfist]], who stood guard alongside [[Vanya]].

A ferocious battle ensued. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]'s [[Frostreaver]] and [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]]'s ice arrows proved highly effective against the fire-aligned creatures, yet the party remained outnumbered by these tenacious demonic canines. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] cast a crushing earth eruption spell that rendered nearly every combatant unconscious—friend and foe alike. Only one hellhound remained barely conscious, along with [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] and [[Gio]], both gravely injured by the indiscriminate force. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] dispatched the sole survivor with a dramatic flourish, declaring "omae wa mou, shindeiru."

The alpha female, hearing her offspring's agonized death cries, entered a berserk rage. She burrowed through the earth, emerging directly beneath [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] and seizing him in her massive jaws, rendering him unconscious. The party rallied immediately, while [[Vanya]] appeared from the adjoining chamber with two large serpents—one crimson red, the other ocean blue—that delivered devastating radiant attacks upon the hellhound. [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] struggled with his unwieldy [[Vajra]], failing to land a strike with the absurdly oversized weapon.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] launched an explosive ice knife that damaged both the target and his allies. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] released a critical shot that inflicted massive damage, leaving the beast clinging to life as it unleashed a final heat wave in desperation. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]] delivered the killing blow, ending the alpha's rampage as the initiates broke into spontaneous celebration of the young patriarch's victory.

After the battle concluded, the party attended to [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]], who had sustained severe injuries while unconscious in the hellhound's jaws. Though the healing restored him to consciousness, his body still bore the savage marks of the alpha's mauling, leaving him in considerable pain as they prepared to continue their journey deeper into the volcanic labyrinth.
