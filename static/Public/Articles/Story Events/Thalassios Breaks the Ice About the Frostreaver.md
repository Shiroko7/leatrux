After most spectators of the arena have left to their corresponding factions to start the process of raiding the [[Infernoheart Citadel]], the new reformed crew of the [[Fulh'ulbrech]] is lagging behind in organization. The conscious of the masters is that they will try to depart as soon as [[Nilo]] breakthrough the adept level. 

Accompanied by [[Grommash Thunderfist]] and followed by [[Fendral]], the party begins to catch up with one another before making plans for the future. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] finally shares a more detailed account of his experience with the [[Frostreaver]] and [[Selune]], a story he had only briefly hinted at to [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] during the [[Trial of Fire]]. He describes how he met her, residing within the [[Frostreaver]], and ultimately fusing with the blade during his foundation establishment ritual. Confessing to the many difficulties he faced, he reveals that [[Selune]] ultimately saved him, gradually merging a part of her consciousness into his being. He now shares many of her core memories as a former member of the [[Storm Tribe]], and suggests that the party could potentially benefit from learning the lightning path.

The party received his unlikely story with raised eyebrows and cautious curiosity. [[Fendral]] thought he was on a heavy acid trip and disregarded the account completely, while the rest listened with intrigued skepticism—particularly when [[Thalassios]] demonstrated his ability to summon the [[Frostreaver]] at will. [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]], bringing out the theme of the lightning path, tells them they could ask [[Lei Zhen]], whom he calls the "Old Lightning", for advice and guidance.

He also reveals that he established his foundation with a magical artifact, something [[Lei Zhen|Old Lightning]] gave him called the [[Vajra]]. He attempts to pull it out from the ground—an extremely difficult task—lifting a huge magical artifact nearly twice his size. He laments that he is not big enough to use it yet, despite [[Nilo]]'s very rational advice of using magic instead of brute force. Grommash ignores this, insisting that it's obviously a weapon so it's meant to be wielded by hand.

As the group prepared to disperse, they left [[Nilo]] to focus on his critical breakthrough. [[Fendral]] offered a cryptic well-wish before departing, being too busy to help [[Nilo]] this time, leaving everyone wondering about the mysterious figure who had been inexplicably following them. The parting question hung in the air: who exactly was this guy?
