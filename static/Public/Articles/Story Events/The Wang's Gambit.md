[[Herng]] collected the promised funding from [[Chromaela Ironheart]]—a modest box containing 2,000 gold pieces. Despite his attempts at conversation, [[Chromaela]] requested he maintain his distance, citing discomfort at his proximity.

Later, [[Gio]] pursued the [[Twilight Plum]], a spiritual fruit granting darkvision. In his enthusiasm, he began bidding at ten thousand gold, oblivious to the fact that the predominantly elven audience already possessed this ability, leaving him with virtually no competition. [[Herng]] spent their remaining funds on the [[Phoenix Blood Orange]] to gain fire resistance. Upon consuming these spiritual fruits, both cultivators collapsed unconscious as their bodies adjusted to the overwhelming influx of spiritual energy.

The auction progressed to servants and slaves, with the party briefly contemplating hiring help for rebuilding the [[Fulh'ulbrech]], though they found the prices low, it wasn't worth the effort. At this juncture, an unexpected merchant stepped forward—[[Wang Dabao|Juan David De la Lluvia]].

Rising to his feet with genuine emotion reflected in his expression, [[Wang Dabao|Juan David]] addressed the audience: "I, [[Wang Dabao|Juan David de la Lluvia]], have a dream. Perhaps it's not as grand as those who aspire to transcend the island, but as a member of the fishermen's guild, I've always yearned to travel beyond the coast, beyond the skies. I love my home, but the path to eternal life makes us abandon many things. This is why this creation excites me so much... this precious treasure can transport hundreds and travel across countless oceans."

His voice grew more emotional, nearly breaking as he continued: "With it, every place I go will be my home, because in it I can have my family, my friends, my tribe! This treasure is capable of such wonders and requires not a single drop of spiritual energy. It's... convenient."

His tone subtly shifted, taking on a more persuasive air: "And today, distinguished cultivators, I have the honor of offering positions on this magnificent vessel! The [[Fulh'ulbrech]] is not just a ship, it's a floating community, a moving home, a palace that defies the waves! There are cabins for cultivators of all paths, meditation spaces with views of the horizon, and training areas to perfect your arts. All this while traveling to new destinations!" With a broad smile and a wink, he concluded: "Reserve your place now on the [[Fulh'ulbrech]], the greatest treasure on the entire island... after [[Wang Dabao]], of course."

The crowd responded with enthusiasm, and bids quickly materialized. The party noted some acquaintances making suspiciously brief bids before dropping out with unnatural timing, but prices steadily rose nonetheless. With over a hundred seats available at starting prices of one thousand gold each, their prospects appeared promising. However, complications arose when undesirable parties secured positions—including [[Liberation Sect]] masters [[Bei Li'er]], [[Sister Mei]], and [[Yu Wei]], merchants [[Yan Li]] and [[Hei Mige]] from the [[Llamarada Tribe]], [[Xie Sulian]] and [[Kazren Serpentsoul]] from the [[Tribe Espora]], and [[Su Yaolan]] from the [[Lluvia Tribe]].

[[Wang Dabao|Juan David]] then escalated his performance, presenting blueprints to clan patriarchs and elders, offering them the opportunity to construct their own battleships for 200,000 gold pieces each. When skepticism emerged about his capability to deliver functional designs, [[Nilo]] attempted to validate the blueprints through [[Durim Stonehammer]], who unfortunately could not read the common script they were written in. [[Nilo]] silently cursed his luck, wishing the unconscious [[Herng]] were available to help.

In a dramatic reveal, [[Wang Dabao|Juan David]] summoned the "handsome and talented dharma armament cultivator" [[Wang Dabao]]—removing his disguise glasses to show they were the same person, merely claiming the puppet was his creation. The crowd stared in disbelief at their uncanny resemblance. Despite continued skepticism about his ability to build even a small-scale model, [[Wang Dabao]] claimed to be the renowned "grand treasure" dharma armament daoist.

What followed was both bizarre and captivating. [[Wang Dabao]] slowly studied his own blueprint, nodding appreciatively and occasionally becoming moved by his own work. The bewildered audience watched as he launched into a dazzling performance, secretly assisted by [[The Serpent|el Serpiente]].

His display showed extraordinary skill, simultaneously manipulating multiple materials from his bag of holding without error. The refined artistry of his technique entranced observers, with [[Durim Stonehammer]] collapsing unconscious from shock. Dharma armament cultivators from all tribes watched in utter disbelief as he completed a miniature ship model over several hours.

The display transformed skepticism into enthusiasm, with factions eagerly competing for blueprint copies. [[Wang Dabao]] found himself showered with unprecedented offers, securing extraordinary profits from what had begun as a desperate gambit.
