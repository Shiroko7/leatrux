While awaiting [[Nilo]]'s emergence from his profound meditation, [[Vanya]] and [[Elira]] found themselves in need of diversion. Their mischievous gazes fell upon [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]], seeing in the triton a perfect subject for their cultivator's curiosity and amusement.

With practiced persuasion born from decades of manipulation, they urged him toward a life-altering decision under the noble pretense of necessity. "We have exhausted all options with [[Nilo]]," they insisted, their voices carrying the weight of medical authority. "We must try every method to provoke a reaction, regardless of consequences."

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] raised a feeble question about whether the [[Netherbreath Fungus]] would truly help his companion at all, but found himself swiftly engulfed in their masterful gaslighting.

"Would you deny [[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]] this chance?" they pressed. "After all, he specifically purchased this fungus for you, did he not? And you spent such an extravagant sum on the [[Manual of Quickness of Action]], depleting our collective resources. Surely this small sacrifice isn't too much to ask. Should he perish, what else would remain to remember him by?"

Like countless disciples before powerful masters, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] silently accepted his fate. He was, after all, no stranger to having his physical form examined and manipulated—the sensation of being probed beyond his nostrils and into his frontal lobe was merely another trial on his cultivation path.

He took the fungus with the stoic dignity of a true cultivator, standing motionless as he submitted to the procedure. Soon, an intense itching sensation bloomed behind his eyes as the [[Netherbreath Fungus]] attached itself to his brain, establishing an electric connection that pulsed between them.

Then came the revelation—scents flooded his consciousness with overwhelming intensity. The metallic tang of blood, the salt of his own perspiration, the floral notes emanating from the masters' hair, even the distant fragrance of grass and trees outside the chamber. After months of olfactory limitations, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] released a deep sigh, simultaneously overwhelmed and relieved by this new sensory dimension.

The party observed his transformation with fascination. Faint, luminescent spores glowed with each breath he took, while the blood vessels in his eyes took on a distinct blue hue. Yet his nose, the subject of so much experimentation, remained stubbornly underdeveloped.

By the following day, a new development manifested—blue hair growing outward from his nostrils, creating the appearance of a most unusual mustache sprouting directly from his nose.
