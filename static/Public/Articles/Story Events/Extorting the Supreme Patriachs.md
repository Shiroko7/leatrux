Though our heroes had successfully retrieved [[Nilo]] from the threshold of death, they faced another pressing trial—the expedition to the [[Infernoheart Citadel]]. Despite possessing considerable wealth still at their disposal, they remained too greedy to part with it willingly. Following [[The Serpent]]'s cunning suggestion, they resolved to approach the supreme patriarchs of [[The Three Great Tribes]] under a façade of begging, while their true intent was clearly extortion.

[[Grommash Thunderfist]] accompanied them as a bargaining piece, as instructed by [[The Serpent|Serpent]]. Borrowing [[Durim Stonehammer]]'s small flying ship, they set course for the [[Lluvia Tribe]], pressing the accelerator to its limit as they battled fierce headwinds. Their journey was abruptly halted mid-air by a powerful wind technique executed by two masters of the [[Lluvia Tribe]].

[[Neraia Wavelight]] stood before them, her slender, mature figure radiating dignity. Her short silver hair framed features that spoke of centuries of cultivation and wisdom. Beside her, the more youthful [[Aquara]] presented a mystical presence, her light purple hair flowing around her like living water, a modest cleavage visible beneath her blue robes, her eyes holding the secrets of the rain essence.

"Do you possess a flying license?" they inquired severely, explaining the considerable expense and scarcity of such privileges on this small island. They warned that [[Durim Stonehammer]]'s license would be revoked should his vehicle continue to be operated so recklessly. Through skillful persuasion, the party secured forgiveness, but as the masters released the wind currents restraining them, catastrophe struck.

The flying boat plummeted into free fall. [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]], with typical impetuousness, made their situation worse by frantically pushing the accelerator rather than seeking a proper solution. "Jump!" he commanded before executing his lightning step technique to safely abandon the plummeting vessel. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]], assessing her companions' survival prospects, seized [[Nilo]] and escaped on her [[Flying Broom]]. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] called upon his druidic powers to ensure his safety, while [[Gio]] relied on his martial prowess to avoid lethal impact.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] attempted to harness his [[Gust of Wind]] to soften his descent, but the technique proved wholly inadequate for this purpose. Mere feet from becoming fish paste, he was instead submerged in water, feeling his bones creak rather than shatter completely.

The sweet floral fragrance of lotus enveloped him as soft arms and a gentle bosom cradled his form. He found himself embraced by a stunning beauty with the unmistakable aura of a master. Her jade-like skin glowed with inner spiritual light, and a cascade of silken hair framed features that seemed carved by heaven's finest artisan. Most captivating was her alluring figure—slender waist giving way to generous hips, her flowing robes doing little to conceal the perfect curves that would drive even immortals to distraction.

Her voice caressed his senses as she admonished, "Please exercise greater care in future endeavors. As an adept, you must lead by example." [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] could only nod in acknowledgment as he regained his footing, his gaze involuntarily following her swaying hips as she departed, each step a rhythm that commanded attention from all who had gathered to investigate the flying mishap.

After tending to [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]]'s substantial injuries, the party regrouped and proceeded to the main palace of the [[Lluvia Tribe]]—a place of unparalleled luxury and beauty. Upon requesting an audience with Supreme Matriarch [[Selene Aeloria]], they were ushered into her chambers. Before anyone could speak, [[The Serpent|Serpent]] loudly proclaimed: "[[Selene]] you degenerate, I know what you have been doing behind everyone's back, taking advantage of those beneath you for your own pleasure! Even mortals are not spared from your uncontrollable lust, you HOE!"

[[Selene Aeloria|Selene]]'s response was immediate and terrible—conjuring a massive tide wave and manifesting a portion of her domain in full alert.

"You dare put a hand on the disciple of [[Lei Zhen]]? I'll have you know that I'm very acqui-bfbabababfbf," the male [[The Serpent|Serpent]] retorted, his words dissolving into unintelligible sounds as currents filled the room. The party felt the crushing pressure of her water domain, while [[The Serpent]] prudently concealed himself, abandoning them to continue negotiations alone.

After venting her anger by toying with the party, [[Selene Aeloria|Selene]] demanded their purpose. They explained their dire need for gold—300,000 pieces, a truly exorbitant sum. She agreed on condition they swear a dao oath never to speak, spread, or reference [[The Serpent|Serpent]]'s slanderous utterances, lest they face destruction in body and soul. When they refused collective accountability, citing their inability to trust their own "stupidity," she relented, accepting individual oaths in exchange for 200,000 gold pieces.

Before departing, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] quietly inquired about the [[Celestial Divine River Tribe]] and their legendary armaments. She revealed they had possessed a mystical boat used to descend the volcano before meeting with calamity, becoming the first tribe to dissolve and merge with the [[Heavenly Tempest Tribe]]. She shared speculations regarding the legend's possible locations.

As they prepared to leave, astonished at their success, [[Selene Aeloria|Selene]] invited [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] to remain for a private tour, having apparently developed an interest in him. Oblivious to her proposition, he declined, expressing his preference to "hang out with the boys" after their long separation.

Forced to walk to the [[Tribe Espora]] due to their ruined vehicle, the party arrived still damp from their earlier mishap. Standing before [[Zarvon Fungart]], [[The Serpent|Serpent]] immediately launched another brazen gambit: "[[Zarvon Fungart|Zarvon]] you rascal, you think you're so clever spreading your spores throughout the island? What would happen if everyone learned you're attempting to mind control them? Your plan would falter! Compensate us for our silence, or watch your scheme collapse!"

The [[Zarvon Fungart|Sovereign]], appearing more lucid than usual with a hint of desolation in his gaze, responded with a cold harrumph. "Indeed, little nieces of the sect. I have failed to properly guide you as your senior..." What followed was hours of torture via his spores, subjecting them to overstimulation, pain, and hallucinations. When it ended, [[Zarvon Fungart|Zarvon]] offered advice regarding their upcoming expedition, explaining that adepts were crucial since higher-level cultivators might cause dangerous instability during deeper descents—the reason supreme patriarchs had withdrawn, leaving masters to lead the lower ranks.

He warned against engaging [[Mu Youqing]], whose presence alone might prematurely awaken the volcano. Finally, he provided 100,000 gold pieces, explaining the [[Tribe Espora]]'s financial constraints due to resources invested in his granddaughter [[Laura Bush]], and his own preparations for the island's opening.

Their final destination was the [[Llamarada Tribe]], where guards watched them carefully as they sought audience with [[Thornor Bramblehelm]]. Before [[The Serpent|Serpent]] could speak, [[Thornor Bramblehelm|Thornor]] hastily presented them with 50,000 platinum pieces in a chest of holding, dismissing them immediately. "See, having a reputation is very important," [[The Serpent|Serpent]] boasted afterward.

The party spent time investigating how these rumors had spread, discovering widespread speculation about [[Nilo]]'s relationship with [[Su Yaolan]]. Tales of his nocturnal visits to the [[Su Clan]] and her frequent appearances at the [[Fulh'ulbrech]] had become common knowledge throughout the island. The [[Liberation Sect]] had been particularly active in spreading these rumors, some members seemingly jealous of [[Nilo]]'s apparent closeness to the jade beauty. Further complicating matters were whispers of [[Nilo]]'s connections to various other women—[[Lime]]'s possessive behavior toward him at public gatherings, [[Bei Li'er]]'s alleged special treatment, and even [[Sister Mei]]'s unusual interest in his medical techniques. These overlapping rumors had created a complex web of speculation that followed [[Nilo]] wherever he went, lending an unexpected weight to his reputation among the island's cultivation society.
