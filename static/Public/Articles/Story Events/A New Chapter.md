The chamber fell into momentary silence as [[Nilo]]'s companions absorbed the significance of his successful breakthrough. Their relief was palpable—a weight lifted after days of anxious vigil.

"[[Nilo]], you've been unmoving for a week," said one of his companions, their voice betraying the depth of concern they had carried.

[[Vanya]] sighed dramatically, her usual sharp demeanor softened by genuine relief. "Heavens, why do you have to give us so much trouble? Why can't you both be normal?" Though her words were cutting, her tone carried an unmistakable undercurrent of affection.

[[Gio]] shuffled forward awkwardly, his reptilian features arranged in what might have been contrition. "[[Nilo]], during my desperation, I attempted to kick the blood but accidentally hit your face. Sorry for that." The tortle's admission carried a simplicity that belied the complex emotions of the past week.

[[Nilo]] glanced around at the gathered faces, surprise evident in his expression. He had not expected such a crowd to await his awakening. "I'm sorry for troubling you. Thanks for helping me, everyone." His voice was thin, still recovering from the ordeal. Then, with growing curiosity, he asked, "Are you still here just for me? Haven't you started the expedition yet?"

[[Marisella]] stepped forward, her eyes warm with affection. "We wouldn't start the expedition without our captain," she responded simply, as if the answer were the most natural thing in the world.

[[Su Yaolan]] approached with graceful steps, her jade-like countenance radiating celestial elegance even in the mundane surroundings of the recovery chamber. "[[Nilo]], congratulations on your breakthrough. I attempted using purifying magic on your heart, trying to wake you up. I hope it wasn't too painful for you."

"I could feel it," [[Nilo]] replied, his eyes meeting hers with genuine gratitude. "You helped me stay focused. I'm thankful for that, Daoist Yaolan."

[[Lime]]'s melodious voice cut through the moment with playful sharpness. "[[Nilo]], I'm glad that you are fine, however, you worry me too much." She adopted the pose and demeanor of an aggrieved wife, as if beaten unjustly before an audience. "How much longer do you plan to cheat on me? I'm afraid you won't live long with so many girls around you."

Though spoken in jest, her words sent ripples of discomfort through the gathering. [[Lime]]'s theatrical performance raised eyebrows among those present, particularly among the women whose relationships with [[Nilo]] were less clearly defined. An awkward tension descended upon the chamber as [[Su Yaolan]], [[Lumina]], [[Zyrenia Serpentsoul]], and several others—[[Connie]], [[Griselle]], and [[Ginevra]], who were deeply indebted to him—found themselves suddenly reevaluating their connections to the newly advanced cultivator.

Even the normally composed [[Lumina]] seemed affected by the atmosphere. "I as well have been keeping close watch on him," she offered, "making sure that no unexpected circumstances happen by reading the celestial energy..." Her voice trailed off as she realized her words might be misconstrued in the current context.

[[Zyrenia Serpentsoul]] was the first to break under the mounting awkwardness. "Uhm, step-younger-brother, Father says to pay him a visit once you're feeling better," she mumbled, her voice barely audible and her face flushed bright red with embarrassment. "I will take my leave for now." The young woman retreated hastily, clearly mortified by the implications of her clan's relationship with [[Nilo]] after her father's imprudent proposal of adoption.

The party's attention collectively shifted to [[Bei Li'er]], their gazes openly questioning her presence. The merchant princess met their silent inquiry with characteristic sharpness. "It's not like I've come to see [[Nilo]]'s dying face or anything," she declared with an indignant "hmph," folding her arms across her chest.

[[Sister Mei]] smoothly intervened, her diplomatic skills evident as she explained, "We just happened to be discussing some things related to the [[Liberation Sect]] when we heard that [[Nilo]]'s blood from the ritual was draining out." She stepped forward, presenting [[Korrin Flameheart|Korrin]]'s robes to cover [[Nilo]], who was still shirtless from the ritual.

The door swung open again as [[Wang Dabao]] entered with [[Grommash Thunderfist]], both calling out congratulations to [[Nilo]]. Their boisterous arrival shifted the atmosphere toward something lighter as [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] launched into an animated account of his "little brother" who would be thrilled to meet them again.

[[Nilo]] frowned in confusion, thinking [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] was referring to [[Fendral]]. His companions quickly corrected him—the "four-eyes guy" from [[Sylas]]'s group whom they had encountered outside the [[Aquagloom Abyss]].

"What's his cultivation level?" someone asked.

[[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] laughed. "He's still in the first level of spiritual energy refining or something like that. The [[Lei Zhen|Old Lightning]] is training him purely in martial arts using a wooden sword. He's a bit slow." He proceeded to describe how [[Elandor]] had been thrown flying toward the peak of the mountain, landing precisely outside their cave. "If that's not fate, I don't even know what fate means."

[[Vanya]] cleared her throat, bringing them back to more practical matters. "Well, now that [[Nilo]] is awake, we only need a little bit more money to cover all the expenses. The resources and rations we need are very hard to come by, especially in the quantities required for a group our size." Her expression grew calculating. "Every master has already given their share. We're just waiting for [[Nilo]] to contribute a few thousand gold pieces—maybe two or three hundred."

A cold calculation ran through the party's minds. They had amassed around six hundred thousand gold pieces from their various ventures. Half of that fortune had already been allocated to repair the [[Fulh'ulbrech]] and secure resources for their long-planned expedition across the seas. Now, the remaining half would be consumed merely to fund their journey to the [[Infernoheart Citadel]]—leaving them without a single coin to their names.

The party members dropped to the ground one by one at this news, like defeated earthworms, the prospect of further financial strain clearly overwhelming.

[[Fendral]], who had been unexpectedly named earlier in the conversation, suddenly said "Hello" as if he had been standing beside the group from the very beginning. "If I were in your position," he offered casually, "I would just ask the three supreme patriarchs for gold, using [[Lei Zhen|Old Lightning]] as cover to help pay for the expenses."

The [[The Serpent|Serpent]], who had been awake for several days, fully agreed with this strategy, telling [[Gio]] that he had ways to "extort—I mean, make them cooperate," so there was no need to worry.

[[Gio]] sighed from his position on the floor. "It's not worth it. Gold just comes and goes very often anyway."

"How can you say that while wallowing like that on the floor?" the [[The Serpent|Serpent]] asked incredulously.

"The problem is," [[Gio]] mumbled into the floorboards, "the gold just leaves."
