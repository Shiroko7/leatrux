[[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]] had prepared for this moment meticulously over weeks, transforming a dark chamber into a ritualistic space of profound spiritual significance. Auxiliary pentagrams drawn in blood surrounded a massive bath filled with carefully collected donations—each drop a testament to his complex journey of survival and cultivation.

The party accompanied by [[Marisella]], [[Elira]], and [[Vanya]] watched with a mixture of anticipation and growing unease. [[Marisella]] commented on the odd interior design, her voice cutting through the heavy atmosphere as [[Nilo]] began removing his clothes, preparing to enter the blood-filled receptacle.

He stepped into the pool slowly, his worn feet first touching the crimson liquid. Carefully following breathing and hand formation techniques learned through countless hours of preparation, [[Nilo]] began manipulating the blood. It responded to his will, floating around his body in an intricate lotus pattern, creating a mesmerizing display that suggested both artistry and profound spiritual potential.

The purified [[Heart Of Shadow]], pulled out almost as an afterthought, became the catalyst for catastrophe. When it slipped from his fingers into the blood pool, the liquid erupted as if heated a hundredfold. [[Nilo]] felt his own blood being drained, his skin shredding like wet paper. A black shadow emerged, shattering the blood-filled container and spraying its thick, disgusting contents across the room.

Shadows began to take human form, each materializing with a chorus of anguished regrets. They revealed the atrocious acts [[Nilo]] had committed to survive, their voices laden with profound resentment. When [[Nilo]] attempted to defend himself, claiming he had no other option, the shadows ruthlessly reprimanded him—particularly for choosing [[Lime]] over everyone else's lives.

[[Vanya]]'s reaction was immediate and devastating. Utterly disgusted, she declared she should have let them die when they first met in the [[Venom Sanctum]]. "Never call me master again," she proclaimed, abandoning [[Nilo]] to his fate. She described his blood tribulation as punishment for being "unforgivable by the heavens."

[[Elira]]'s response was more heartbreaking. Weeping uncontrollably, she asked [[Nilo]] why he would commit such vile acts, why he had deceived her after everything they had done for him. Though [[Nilo]] knew he had confessed before, she seemed to have forgotten, and his attempts to persuade her fell on deaf ears.

Though incomprehensible to [[Nilo]], these cryptic words preceded [[Elira]]'s body being torn apart. A massive wraith emerged, launching itself directly at [[Nilo]].

The battle that followed was nothing short of apocalyptic. Shadows attacked with murderous intent, their claws seeking to tear apart [[Nilo]]'s companions. [[Marisella]] fell first, her neck brutally ripped open as whispers of "come join us" echoed through the chamber.

[[Gio]] struggled to land attacks, unable to believe the horror unfolding. [[Thalassios]] slashed repeatedly, [[Laura]] attempted to maintain healing tides, and [[Herng]] hurled fireballs that the shadows effortlessly dodged. When he produced a magic item capable of summoning daylight, it seemed a futile gesture against the overwhelming darkness.

Driven by [[Su Yaolan]]'s teachings of integration, [[Nilo]] attempted to absorb the shadows using the [[Heart Of Shadow]], and then risked everything by attempting to consume them directly with his [[Heart Crushing Grip]]. His companions watched in horror as he pushed himself beyond reasonable limits.

[[Thalassios]] tried to heal [[Nilo]], but the radiant spell only pushed him into unconsciousness. As [[Nilo]] fell, his dark dragonmark began spreading across his body. From his neck, a thick shadow emerged—a medium-sized aberrant creature with draconic features composed of pure darkness.

Its presence was overwhelming, radiating a power far beyond the previous shadows. [[Laura]] and [[Gio]] could only wail in primal, animalistic fear, witnessing [[Nilo]]'s terrifying transformation.
