After [[Wang Dabao]]'s remarkable performance, the party contemplated their options—take the money and run, abandon their ship plans entirely, or construct a new vessel. [[Harpy]] offered a simpler alternative: "Why not just travel in a foldable magical boat? It could fit six people with your luck." As they worried about navigation and [[Grommash Thunderfist]]'s whereabouts, fate intervened with dramatic timing.

A thunderclap shook the open-air coliseum as a figure strode through the entrance—[[Grommash Thunderfist]] himself, blue electricity dancing across his green skin. The crowd instinctively retreated as he reached the arena's center and proclaimed:

"He who understands lightning not only masters the sky but also the power hidden in the earth's entrails! Lightning is not merely a destructive force—it is the manifestation of celestial will. Whoever controls lightning controls the flow of destiny itself, defying the heavens and subjugating the earth!"

Spectators exchanged nervous glances as [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] continued his declaration:

"Only one who merges with lightning, becoming one with its fury and light, will hold the power to shape the world to his will. For lightning not only illuminates the sky—it also reveals the true path to immortality!"

Tribal guards advanced with weapons drawn. One from the Espora tribe confronted the crew: "You arrived with him on the island! Are you involved in this madness? Help stop him before the [[Zarvon Fungart|soverign]] sovereign finds out!"

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Laura Cassini|Laura]] led their approach, with others following cautiously to avoid implication. Surprisingly, [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] greeted [[Nilo]] warmly, claiming to have "heard great things" about him—acting as though they were meeting for the first time despite months together aboard the [[Fulh'ulbrech]]. 

As tension mounted, [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] declared: "I've come to challenge the one known as the son of the great... mist! I want to show him what I've learned during my exile!"

"The lightning path is forbidden!" shouted an elder from the crowd. "It threatens the entire island!"

Just as violence seemed inevitable, a sudden breeze swept through the coliseum. From the blue sky descended a figure, floating majestically on air currents—[[Quill Rest]], his empty sleeve fluttering as a reminder of his sacrifice.

"Stand back," he commanded the guards with quiet authority. "I will handle this matter."

[[Quill Rest|Quill]] regarded [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] with genuine curiosity: "Thunderfist... an imposing name. Like a fish aspiring to become a dragon. Let's see if you're worthy of such a title."

[[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] responded with a wild smile: "I've been reckless in the past. I hope you can forgive my conduct. But today, I've come to return the punch from that time."

[[Quill Rest|Quill]] ascended higher, creating a clear disadvantage for his opponent: "Hmm, you wish to prove yourself here before everyone?"

[[Herng]] awakened just in time, delighted to see his old friend. [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] greeted him and [[Gio]], noting, "You've both changed much since we last met." The growing audience followed them to the arena, anticipating the impending clash.
  
The battle began with [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] at a disadvantage, attempting lightning-enhanced jumps that barely grazed his airborne opponent. [[Quill Rest|Quill]] dodged with minimal movement, taunting: "Is this all you've learned?" before summoning a dense fog to blanket the arena.

"Damn, the old man never mentioned this bastard could fly," muttered [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] as visibility diminished.

From within the mist, [[Quill Rest|Quill]]'s voice seemed to come from everywhere: "In the fog, wind finds its perfect form." [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] received blows from multiple directions, unable to locate his attacker.

Suddenly, the half-orc paused, closing his eyes in concentration. "Remember the old man's words, trust his teachings—lightning always seeks the path of least resistance," he whispered before exclaiming: "I CAN SEE YOU!"

With precise movement, he released a discharge that electrified the water particles in the fog. For an instant, the entire mist illuminated, revealing [[Quill Rest|Quill]]'s silhouette. [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]'s strategy evolved as he began charging the mist with static electricity, transforming his opponent's advantage into his weapon. Small discharges connected between clouds, forming an electrical storm above the coliseum.

"Impressive," acknowledged [[Quill Rest|Quill]], abandoning the protection of his fog.

During this display, [[Lime]] casually asked [[Nilo]] about the blue mark on [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]'s chest: "That mark looks quite familiar to yours. He clearly has lightning path affinity." [[Nilo]] replied that "well, he clearly has affinity with other things," revealing that [[Laura Cassini|Laura]] also possessed such a mark, sharing this confidence only because he trusted [[Lime]].

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] watched with a growing headache as the combatants' techniques felt strangely familiar from [[Selune]]'s memories. "Lightning path cultivators have a clear advantage against many water path techniques," he observed to his companions.  

Seeing [[Wang Dabao]] collecting wagers, [[Nilo]] approached [[Kazren Serpentsoul]] to bet on [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]'s victory. The stakes were extraordinary—if [[Nilo]] lost, he would become [[Kazren]]'s adopted son; if he won, he would reclaim the [[Serpent's Molt of the Abyss]]. Unwilling to risk adoption, [[Nilo]] counteroffered his companions instead. [[Kazren]] attempted to include his daughters as additional commodities before finally accepting the wager.

As the duel reached its climax, both combatants prepared their ultimate techniques. [[Quill Rest|Quill]] generated a compressed wind vortex while [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]] formed a pure electricity lance.

"Sky-Splitting Spear!" exclaimed [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]].

"Eternal Wind Spiral," answered [[Quill Rest|Quill]].

Before these forces could collide, a wall of green spores materialized between them, absorbing both attacks. [[Zarvon Fungart]] advanced with unusual lucidity, launching a deadly poisonous spore attack toward [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]].

A violet discharge intercepted and dispersed the attack as a white-haired, scarred elder appeared beside [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]. "Your manners remain terrible, [[Zarvon Fungart|Zarvon]]," said [[Lei Zhen]], his presence radiating forgotten power.

[[Zarvon Fungart|Zarvon]]'s eyes narrowed with hatred. "[[Lei Zhen]]. I thought you'd died with the rest of your tribe."

"Some lightning never extinguishes," came the simple response as he placed a protective hand on [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]'s shoulder.
