Having secured their ill-gotten bounty through shameless extortion, the party turned their attention to preparations for their perilous journey. They invested in essential equipment—fire, ice, and poison resistance rings being foremost among their purchases. After much deliberation, they allocated 300,000 gold to finance the construction of a secondary vessel capable of accommodating a crew of thirty, devoted 500,000 gold to expedition expenses, and distributed 20,000 gold to each party member.

On the eve of their departure, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] felt compelled to settle his karmic accounts. Gathering the belongings they had looted from [[Zara]] and [[Llyrith]], he set out for the [[Lluvia Tribe]] accompanied by [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] and [[Grommash Thunderfist]]. The half-orc, eager to explore the magnificent palace of [[Selene Aeloria]] during his free time, departed ahead of them with innocent enthusiasm.

[[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] approached [[Bei Li'er]]'s establishment seeking information about [[Zara]], whom he recalled seeing there previously. When he inquired with [[Dhalia]], she initially mistook him for a client, weighing whether to provide guidance before realizing the misunderstanding. With an apologetic smile, she redirected him to [[Lady Elowen]]'s host club, suggesting it as the likeliest daytime location for his quarry.

There, [[Fin]] greeted them, overseeing the establishment while [[Lady Elowen]] attended the [[Infernoheart Citadel expedition]]. Like [[Dhalia]], [[Fin]] assumed [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] sought to book [[Zara]]'s company. During their conversation, [[Fin]] mentioned that the club had recently experienced unprecedented success by incorporating female staff, noting how the beautiful women of the [[Lluvia Tribe]] had proven exceptionally popular among the men of the [[Liberation Sect]]. Indebted to the party for past assistance, [[Fin]] discreetly informed [[Zara]] and directed [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] to her chamber.

[[Zara]] welcomed him with an effortlessly provocative demeanor that seemed more instinct than intention. Her long, ashen blonde hair fell loosely around her shoulders, delicate bangs framing crimson eyes that smoldered like dying embers. The moisture on her skin and the silken quality of her hair betrayed a recent bath, her modest single-piece robe wrapped loosely around her form, occasionally shifting to reveal glimpses of pale skin. Yet beneath this surface allure lay a deep melancholy—a profound loneliness that clung to her like morning mist, impossible to dispel despite her practiced grace. Each movement, each word carried this dual nature—the outward beauty of a fire cultivator and the inward sorrow of one who had long walked solitary paths.

With characteristic bluntness, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] announced his purpose and presented her belongings, which she accepted with simple gratitude. When questioned about [[Llyrith]], whom [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] had struggled to locate despite extensive inquiries throughout the tribe, [[Zara]] admitted they hadn't communicated since the incident but suggested she might have joined a squadron alongside [[Grimmy]].

As [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] prepared to depart, [[Zara]] assured him she had no intention of revealing the events of their past confrontation—such disclosure would yield her no benefit. She entrusted him with an item for [[Grimmy]], an undersized hand-made shirt bordered with his name, reminiscent of a mother's gift. The implication of [[Grimmy]]'s recent visits hung in the air, though a shadow crossed her features as she mentioned that after the volcano's eruption and the island's opening, she doubted their paths would cross again. This farewell gift carried the weight of permanent separation. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] departed, declining [[Zara]]'s thinly veiled invitation to linger.

Meanwhile, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] engaged [[Fin]] in conversation about [[Lady Elowen]], her squadron, and the promised yet undelivered gift of tailored robes. To his surprise, [[Fin]] revealed that all commissioned garments had indeed been completed—comfortable, aesthetically pleasing attire of the highest quality, specifically designed for each party member.

As [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] received these robes, unexpected fragments of [[Selune]]'s memories surfaced. The vivid image of [[Lady Elowen]] materializing from thousands of years past created a profound cognitive dissonance, challenging his understanding of elven cultivator lifespans. Even [[Selene Aeloria]] and [[Lei Zhen]], the last patriarch of the [[Storm Tribe]], belonged to different generations than those in these memories, raising disturbing questions about [[Lady Elowen]]'s true nature.

Through these ancestral recollections, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] discovered [[Lady Elowen]]'s divine ability to extend her lifespan through prolonged hibernation, awakening only during cosmically favorable periods as determined by her cadre of star-reading wizards. In earlier eras, she had eschewed entertainment enterprises, dedicating herself instead to celestial energy interpretation, nurturing rising talents, and engaging fellow masters in the strategic game of go while exchanging cultivation insights. Her relationship with [[Selune]], though not intimate, had been formal and supportive, with [[Lady Elowen]] advocating for [[Selune]]'s elevation to supreme matriarch of the [[Storm Tribe]].

Reunited, [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] and [[Public/Articles/PCs/Thalassios Euphasath|Thalassios]] proceeded to the palace to inquire after [[Grommash Thunderfist|Grommash]]. Their innocent questions were met with alarmed hostility from the guards, who accused them of "actively courting death" by broaching such matters before unceremoniously ejecting them from the premises.

Upon returning to the [[Fulh'ulbrech]], they found the vessel buzzing with pre-departure activity as expedition members bid farewell to loved ones. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Herng Azurit|Herng]] embraced [[Marisella]], admonishing her to take care and avoid death—words she noted should rightfully be hers to speak. [[Lime]] fussed over [[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]]'s robes with wifely attention, warning him against further recklessness, saying she won't be there to cover his deeds—a remark laden with implications about past actions she had concealed on his behalf. [[Public/Articles/PCs/Nilo|Nilo]], uncharacteristically introspective and subdued, acknowledged her concern with a silent nod.

Around them, the intense gazes of female initiates followed [[Nilo]]'s every movement, their interest hardly disguised. Rumor lovers strategically positioned themselves within earshot, eagerly cataloging each interaction, every nuanced expression, ready to disseminate fresh gossip throughout the island's cultivation circles.

The poignant farewells were abruptly shattered by [[Wang Dabao]]'s outlandish antics. The rotund merchant engaged in an elaborate goodbye with his own puppet, [[Wang Dabao|Juan David]], talking to himself with extraordinary shamelessness. His self-complimentary monologue continued unabated as onlookers exchanged bewildered glances, marveling at the boundless depths of one man's impropriety.
